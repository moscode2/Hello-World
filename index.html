About me

Computer scientist and developer

I'm Moses, a full stack developer in Nairobi. I hold a Diploma in Computer Science from The Co-operative University of Kenya (2025) and a Full Stack Web Development certificate from Edureka (2024).

On the front end I work with React and TypeScript. On the back end I use Node.js and Python, with MongoDB or PostgreSQL for data. I like projects that real people use: a news platform, a photo album that wedding guests can add to, an interactive résumé.
